# References (high‑level)

Primary references are maintained in the paper LaTeX sources and the bibliographies.
This repository includes code implementations of standard methods; key anchors include:

- Kuramoto (1975); Strogatz (2000); Acebrón et al. (2005)
- Boashash (1992) for instantaneous frequency/phase foundations
- Theiler et al. (1992); Schreiber & Schmitz (2000) for surrogate data
- Hänggi et al. (1990) for Kramers/reaction‑rate overview
- Gammaitoni et al. (1998) for stochastic resonance review
- Pikovsky et al. (2001); Arenas et al. (2008) for synchronization background
